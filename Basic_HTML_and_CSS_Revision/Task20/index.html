Desert Plants
Desert plants are hardy species that survive extreme heat and very little water through specialized physical and behavioral adaptations.
Cacti
Cacti are succulent plants belonging to the family Cactaceae that are specially adapted to survive in dry, arid environments.
    Features
  • Water Storage
  • Areoles
  • Spines instead of Leaves
  • Night Photosynthesis
Succulents
Succulents are resilient plants with thick, fleshy leaves or stems designed to store water and survive in dry conditions.
    Features
  • Water Storage
  • Diversity

The Joshua tree (Yucca brevifolia) is an iconic, slow-growing succulent plant native to the Mojave Desert of the southwestern United States.
